What this actually is

AMFI publishes scheme identity (scheme code, ISIN, name), Total Expense Ratio, and Average AUM as three separate datasets, keyed two different ways — TER by NSDL's own scheme code, everything else by AMFI's scheme code. This project fetches all three directly from AMFI's own endpoints, resolves the identifier mismatch, and republishes one merged, AMFI-scheme-code-keyed dataset covering every plan and option of every scheme — not just Direct Growth, which is what most public sources limit themselves to.

What makes it different

  • No fabricated data. A field AMFI hasn't published for a scheme, or that couldn't be confidently matched, is simply omitted — never filled in with a guess or a zero.
  • Full universe, not just Direct Growth. Every plan and option variant is included, since downstream consumers may need Regular plans, IDCW options, or other variants, not only the Direct-Growth subset most public sources limit themselves to.
  • Sourced directly from AMFI, not a third-party aggregator — no additional layer of staleness or reformatting risk between AMFI's own disclosure and this dataset.
  • Free and open. No login, no account, no tracking, no subscription.

Who this is for

Built for anyone who needs to join a fund's cost (TER) and size (AUM) to its identity (AMFI scheme code, ISIN) without maintaining their own AMFI scraper and NSDL-to-AMFI matching logic. This is the data behind MF Quant Analyzer's Efficiency scoring, and is published standalone since the matching problem it solves is useful independent of that specific ranking methodology.

How it works

  1. An automated weekly pipeline pulls AMFI's own scheme master (NAVAll.txt), TER disclosure endpoint, and scheme-wise AAUM endpoint directly.
  2. TER's NSDL-keyed records are matched to AMFI scheme codes by normalizing fund names on both sides (stripping plan/option words, historical-name parentheticals, and known AMC renames), falling back to a manual override list for cases the automatic logic can't resolve confidently.
  3. AUM joins directly by AMFI scheme code — no matching needed.
  4. The merged result is republished weekly as JSON and CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Is this free?

Yes, entirely. No account, no login, no paid tier, no data collection. Download the JSON or CSV and it's yours.

Why isn't every scheme's TER or AUM populated?

Two reasons: AMFI may not have published AUM for a scheme in the latest period (e.g. a very new or very small scheme), or the TER matching logic couldn't confidently resolve that scheme's NSDL-keyed TER record to an AMFI code. Either way, the field is simply absent rather than guessed.

Why does this include Regular plans and IDCW options, not just Direct Growth?

Most public sources limit themselves to Direct Growth. This dataset covers every plan/option variant of every scheme, since downstream consumers may need the full picture — a Regular plan's TER, for instance, differs meaningfully from its Direct-plan counterpart.

How often is it updated?

Weekly, via an automated pipeline that pulls AMFI's latest published data and republishes both the JSON and CSV.

Is the source code available?

The data-fetching and matching pipeline is maintained privately; the published data (this repo, updated weekly) is public and free to use, under LessOneLabs on GitHub.

Is this affiliated with AMFI, SEBI, or any fund house?

No. This project has no affiliation with any Asset Management Company, SEBI, or AMFI. It uses AMFI's publicly published data as an input, nothing more.
